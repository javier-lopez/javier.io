{%- comment -%} Posts-per-year bar chart, rendered above the archive list. Why it exists: the archive is 188 entries over 18 years presented as one flat reverse-chronological list, so 2010 — the most prolific year, 36 posts — sits ~170 rows below the fold and is effectively unreachable. The bars give that shape a surface and every year a direct link. Pure Liquid + CSS, no script: each bar's height is its share of the busiest year, written as an inline percentage at build time. Bars are so they work without JavaScript and are keyboard reachable; the anchors are the #y- ids the archive sections already need. Usage: {% include year-histogram.html posts=site.posts %} {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign hist = include.posts | group_by_exp: "post", "post.date | date: '%Y'" -%} {%- assign by_size = hist | sort: "size" -%} {%- assign busiest = by_size | last -%} {%- assign peak = busiest.size -%} {%- assign chron = hist | sort: "name" -%}
    {%- for g in chron -%} {%- assign pct = g.size | times: 100 | divided_by: peak -%}
  1. {{ g.size }} {{ g.name | slice: 2, 2 }}
    2. {%- endfor -%}
