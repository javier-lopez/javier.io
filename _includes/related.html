{%- comment -%} A post may pin its own related list with a `related:` front-matter array of post URLs — curation wins on essays. A URL that matches no post leaves an HTML comment in the output instead of failing silently. Everything else gets tag-overlap related: the 3 newest posts sharing at least one tag, falling back to the newest posts in the same language. Deliberately NOT site.related_posts — with lsi off it degrades to "the 10 newest posts" (every page shows the same list), and with lsi on this corpus it pairs essays with old weekly-notes posts. {%- endcomment -%}
