## {{ page.title }}

###### {{ page.date | date_to_string }}

[![][154]][155]

So, we had our second UGJ here, sweeet! n_n!, this time we couldn’t reach as much people as the last one, at least not locally, but we had some remote guests in our irc channel #ugj-df (yes @rcart, @fitoshido, @akemi, @cesar..) which help us to made a really great event, logs are available [here][156] (in spanish). It started at 13hr UTC-6 and finished 5 hours later n0n/, we did testing of the latest isos, [fixed][157] bugs and [forwarded][158] them to debian we also created documentation. To make it funner we gave away mugs and t-shirts for the more active users which made a nice competition, at the end I truly believe that.., we’ll participate in another one!!!! \n_____n/, so we’ll be looking forward for it!, thanks to all who made it possible!
