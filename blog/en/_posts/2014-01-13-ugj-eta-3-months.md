## {{ page.title }}

###### {{ page.date | date_to_string }}

This is a kindly reminder of the [Ubuntu Global Jam][303], a world wide event
intended to collaborate as community around Ubuntu. This year it will be
organized from **Friday 4th April** 2014 to **Sunday 6th April** 2014 and
right now it’s a great time to start defining where yours will be hosted and
what activities you’ll do.

In the ubuntu-mx team, ours will be hosted on Saturday 5th April 2014, and
we’ve already started pushing publicity through our main media channels, so
[should you do!][304]

Happy Jamming =D!
