Update: [ubuntu loco games 2013 – update](/blog/en/2013/01/09/ubuntu-loco-games-2013-update.html)

The ubuntu-mx team is still celebrating its recent approval status and one way
to do it is organizating the first edition of the Ubuntu Loco Games, the idea
is simple know each other and have fun at the same time, interested?, join us!

From [9:00am CST, February 9 to 4:00pm CST, February 10][211], we’ll match
teams members in a mortal combat of [AssaultCube][212], [Battle for Wesnoth
][213]& [Urban Terror][214], so spread the news and add your team and your
ubuntu warriors to:

<http://pad.ubuntu.com/LocoGames>

If you are not sure which one is your Ubuntu Loco Team, look for it at:
<https://web.archive.org/web/20230822165251/https://loco.ubuntu.com/>

![][215]

The information of the formal server will be delivered 10 minutes before the
event at [@ubuntumx][216] and #ubuntu-mx =)
