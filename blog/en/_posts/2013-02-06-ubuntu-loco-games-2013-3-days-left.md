###### {{ page.date | date_to_string }}

We’re only 3 days left from the Ubuntu Loco Games =)!, are you already
registered?, if not run to do it!, Inscriptions will close in February 8th

  * <http://pad.ubuntu.com/LocoGames>

You don’t need to be the most wild warrior to enter (although that may help),
any person can join us, look around for the [loco team][228] which you belong
to and you’re done, you can have fun with us.

We’ll be playing 3 of the most successful games in the Ubuntu ecosystem: Urban
Terror, Battle for Wesnoth and Assaultcube, and you can start practicing right
away!
