The **Ubuntu Global Jam** is an incredible opportunity for the Ubuntu
community to unite together around a weekend generally one month before the
release of Ubuntu. However if you’re not part of any ubuntu LoCo team for any
reason (I know there are several of you reading) you won’t be able to organize
them.

Personally I think any person being or not part of any LoCo Team should be
able to announce where he/she is gonna sit down and hack on ubuntu =), however
in the meantime, you can look at the current list and assist to one near from
you, or just hangout with your local friends. **Ubuntu is a state of mind**!
